Privacy Policy

RAILQUICK PRIVATE LIMITED

Effective Date: 6 November 2026 • Website: https://www.railquickapp.com / railquickapp.in

RailQuick ("RailQuick", "Company", "we", "our", or "us") is operated by RailQuick Private Limited and is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of its users.

RailQuick is a railway passenger-focused essentials delivery platform designed to enable passengers to order essential products during their train journeys and receive deliveries at their seats or designated railway stations, subject to service availability.

Our initial commercial operations are planned for selected railway stations in Delhi, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store and protect personal information when users access our website, application, register on our platform, participate in pilot programs or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with RailQuick, we may collect the following information:

A. Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address

B. Railway and Delivery Information

  • Train PNR Number, where required
  • Train Number and Train Name
  • Coach and Seat Number
  • Boarding and Destination Stations
  • Journey Date
  • Delivery Location and Contact Details

C. Order Information

  • Products Ordered
  • Order ID
  • Transaction Reference
  • Order Amount
  • Delivery Status
  • Refund and Cancellation Information
  • Customer Feedback and Support Requests

D. Payment Information

When payment services become operational, payments will be processed through authorised third-party payment service providers, including PhonePe Payment Gateway.

We may receive and maintain relevant transaction information, including:

  • Payment Transaction ID
  • Payment Status
  • Payment Amount
  • Payment Date and Time
  • Refund Status

RailQuick does not intend to collect or store customers' UPI PINs, card CVVs or confidential payment authentication credentials. Such credentials are handled by the relevant payment service providers.

E. Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Information
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Website Usage Data
  • Cookies and Analytics Data

2. Purpose of Information Collection

We collect and use personal information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Managing registrations and waitlists.
  • Providing railway passenger-focused essentials delivery services.
  • Verifying train journey and delivery information where necessary.
  • Coordinating orders and deliveries at designated railway stations.
  • Processing payments and refunds.
  • Sending order confirmations and delivery notifications.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions.
  • Improving our website and application.
  • Conducting pilot testing and service validation.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

3. Payment Processing

RailQuick intends to facilitate online payments through authorised third-party payment gateway providers, including PhonePe Payment Gateway.

When customers make payments:

  • Transactions are processed by the relevant payment gateway.
  • Relevant transaction information may be shared with payment gateways, banks and authorised financial institutions.
  • RailQuick may receive payment confirmation and transaction reference details.
  • Eligible refunds will be processed according to our Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable payment provider procedures.

RailQuick does not independently store customers' confidential payment authentication credentials.

4. Information Sharing

RailQuick does not sell, rent or trade customers' personal information for commercial purposes.

Information may be shared with authorised third parties where necessary, including:

  • Delivery and Operational Partners: For coordinating and completing customer orders.
  • Payment Service Providers: Including PhonePe Payment Gateway and relevant financial institutions.
  • Technology Providers: Hosting, cloud infrastructure, databases and security providers.
  • Communication Providers: For OTPs, SMS, emails and notifications.
  • Analytics Providers: For understanding platform usage and improving performance.
  • Government Authorities: Where legally required.

Information sharing will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Data Security

RailQuick implements reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

These measures may include:

  • Secure data storage.
  • Access restrictions.
  • Authentication controls.
  • Encrypted communication where supported.
  • Security monitoring.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

RailQuick retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, manage accounts, process orders, handle refunds, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website and application may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website traffic.
  • Analyse platform performance.
  • Enhance security and user experience.

Users may manage cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

RailQuick may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, communication, analytics and other operational purposes.

Our Platform may also contain links to external websites. Such services operate under their respective privacy policies.

RailQuick is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites.

9. User Rights and Choices

Users may contact RailQuick to:

  • Request information about their personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Raise privacy-related complaints or concerns.

Requests may be submitted through our official support email (support@railquick.in).

We will address such requests in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Pre-Launch and Pilot Operations

RailQuick is currently preparing its platform and operational infrastructure for the launch of its railway essentials delivery services.

During the pre-launch and pilot stages, we may collect information for registrations, waitlists, product testing, pilot participation, customer communication and service validation.

Commercial ordering, payment collection and delivery services will commence when the relevant services are officially launched and made available to customers.

Our initial operational focus is on selected railway stations in Delhi, India. Service availability, product selection and delivery locations may vary depending on operational readiness and station availability.

RailQuick Private Limited reserves the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Updated versions will be published on this page along with the revised effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
