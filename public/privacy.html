Privacy Policy

RAILQUICK PRIVATE LIMITED

Effective Date: 6 November 2026 • Website: https://www.railquick.in • Contact: support@railquick.in

RailQuick Private Limited ("RailQuick", "Company", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our users.

RailQuick is a railway passenger-focused essentials delivery platform that aims to enable passengers to order essential products during their train journeys and receive deliveries at their seats or designated railway stations, subject to service availability.

Our initial service operations are planned for selected railway stations in Delhi, India.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, and protect personal information when users visit our website, use our application, register on our platform, participate in pilot programs, or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with RailQuick, we may collect the following information:

A. Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address

B. Railway and Delivery Information

  • Train PNR Number, where required
  • Train Number and Train Name
  • Coach and Seat Number
  • Boarding and Destination Stations
  • Journey Date and Relevant Train Schedule Information
  • Delivery Location and Contact Details

C. Order Information

  • Products ordered
  • Order ID and Transaction Reference
  • Order Amount
  • Delivery Status
  • Refund and Cancellation Information
  • Customer Feedback and Support Requests

D. Payment Information

When our payment facility becomes operational, payments will be processed through authorised third-party payment service providers, including PhonePe Payment Gateway.

We may receive and maintain relevant transaction information, such as:

  • Payment Transaction ID
  • Payment Status
  • Payment Amount
  • Payment Date and Time
  • Refund Status

RailQuick does not intend to collect or store customers' UPI PINs, card CVVs, or other confidential payment authentication credentials. Such payment authentication is handled by the relevant payment service provider.

E. Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP Address
  • Browser Type
  • Device Information
  • Operating System
  • Website Usage Information
  • Cookies and Analytics Data

2. Purpose of Collecting Information

We collect and use information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts.
  • Providing railway passenger-focused essentials delivery services.
  • Verifying train journey and delivery information where necessary.
  • Coordinating orders and deliveries at designated railway stations.
  • Processing payments and refunds.
  • Sending order confirmations, transaction notifications and delivery updates.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Preventing fraudulent transactions and unauthorised activities.
  • Improving our website, application and customer experience.
  • Conducting pilot testing and service validation.
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

3. Payment Processing

RailQuick intends to integrate third-party payment gateway services, including PhonePe Payment Gateway, to facilitate secure online payments.

When customers make payments through our platform:

  • Payment transactions will be processed by the relevant payment gateway.
  • Payment-related information may be shared with the payment gateway, banks and other authorised financial service providers to complete transactions.
  • RailQuick may receive transaction references and payment confirmation details.
  • Refunds, where applicable, will be processed according to our applicable Refund and Cancellation Policy and the payment provider's procedures.

RailQuick does not independently process or store customers' confidential payment authentication credentials.

4. Information Sharing

RailQuick does not sell, rent, or trade customers' personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

However, information may be shared with authorised parties when necessary for providing our services, including:

  • Delivery and Operational Partners: To coordinate and complete customer orders and deliveries.
  • Payment Service Providers: Including PhonePe Payment Gateway, banks and relevant financial institutions for processing transactions.
  • Technology Service Providers: Hosting, cloud infrastructure, database, security and application service providers.
  • Communication Providers: For sending OTPs, SMS, emails and order notifications.
  • Analytics Providers: To understand platform performance and improve user experience.
  • Government and Regulatory Authorities: Where disclosure is required under applicable law or a valid legal request.

Such sharing will be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Data Security

RailQuick takes reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect users' personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, misuse, disclosure or loss.

These measures may include:

  • Secure data storage and access restrictions.
  • Encrypted communication where supported.
  • Authentication and access controls.
  • Monitoring and security measures.
  • Restricted access to customer information.

However, no electronic transmission or internet-based storage system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

RailQuick retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Account and service management.
  • Order fulfilment and delivery records.
  • Payment reconciliation and refunds.
  • Customer support and dispute resolution.
  • Legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website and application may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality.
  • Remember user preferences.
  • Understand website traffic and usage.
  • Analyse platform performance.
  • Improve user experience and security.

Users may manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. Certain website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

RailQuick may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, communication, analytics and other operational purposes.

Our platform may also contain links to external websites.

These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies and terms. RailQuick is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites or service providers.

9. User Rights and Choices

Users may contact RailQuick to:

  • Request information about their personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Raise concerns regarding the processing or handling of their personal information.

Requests can be submitted through our official support email (support@railquick.in).

We will review and respond to such requests in accordance with applicable laws.

10. Pre-Launch and Pilot Operations

RailQuick is currently preparing its platform and operational infrastructure for the launch of its railway essentials delivery services.

During the pre-launch and pilot stages, we may collect information for registrations, waitlists, product testing, pilot participation, customer communication and service validation.

Actual commercial orders, payment collection and delivery services will commence when the relevant services are officially launched and made available to customers.

Our initial operational focus is on railway passengers travelling through selected railway stations in Delhi. Service availability, product selection and delivery locations may vary depending on operational readiness and station availability.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RailQuick Private Limited reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with the revised effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

12. Contact Information

For privacy-related questions, requests, complaints or concerns, please contact:

RailQuick Private Limited
Website: https://www.railquick.in
Email: support@railquick.in

We will make reasonable efforts to address privacy-related inquiries within an appropriate timeframe.
