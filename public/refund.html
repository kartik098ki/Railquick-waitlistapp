Refund & Cancellation Policy

RAILQUICK PRIVATE LIMITED

Effective Date: 6 November 2026 • Website: https://www.railquickapp.com / railquickapp.in

At RailQuick, we aim to provide a convenient and reliable essentials delivery experience for railway passengers.

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how order cancellations, payment failures, refunds and eligible complaints are handled.

This policy applies to orders placed through the RailQuick website or application once commercial services become operational.

1. Order Cancellation by Customers

Customers may request cancellation of an order subject to its fulfilment status.

  • Before order acceptance: Customers may request cancellation before the order is accepted by the vendor or fulfilment team.
  • After order acceptance: Cancellation requests will be reviewed based on the preparation, procurement and dispatch status.
  • After dispatch: Orders may not be eligible for cancellation once dispatched, except where required by applicable law or where an eligible service or product issue arises.

Customers should contact our support team as soon as possible for cancellation requests.

2. Cancellation by RailQuick

RailQuick reserves the right to cancel an order in situations including:

  • Product unavailability.
  • Vendor or fulfilment partner unavailability.
  • Incorrect or incomplete delivery information.
  • Train delays, cancellations or operational restrictions.
  • Railway station access or delivery restrictions.
  • Technical issues or payment verification failures.
  • Circumstances that make delivery impossible.

Where payment has already been collected, eligible refunds will be initiated through the applicable payment method.

3. Refund Eligibility

Customers may be eligible for a refund in situations including:

  • Order cancelled by RailQuick.
  • Successful payment but order not confirmed.
  • Duplicate payment for the same order.
  • Payment deducted but order not created.
  • Order not delivered due to RailQuick's or its fulfilment partner's inability to complete delivery.
  • Incorrect or materially different products delivered.
  • Damaged or defective products, where applicable.
  • Other circumstances where a refund is required under applicable consumer protection laws.

Each request will be reviewed based on the order and transaction details.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not ordinarily be available in situations such as:

  • Customer provides incorrect train, coach, seat or contact information.
  • Customer is unavailable at the agreed delivery location.
  • Customer changes their mind after an order has been dispatched.
  • Delivery cannot be completed because of inaccurate information supplied by the customer.

These exclusions will not apply where a refund is required under applicable law or where the failure is attributable to RailQuick or its fulfilment partners.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will generally be initiated through the original payment method used for the transaction.

Depending on the payment provider, bank or financial institution:

  • Refund processing may take approximately 5–7 business days after initiation.
  • Actual crediting time may vary.
  • Customers may be asked to provide their Order ID or Transaction ID for verification.

For payment-related issues, customers should contact our support team with the relevant transaction reference.

6. Failed and Duplicate Transactions

If money is deducted from your account but the order is not successfully confirmed:

  • Check your order status on the RailQuick Platform.
  • Contact our customer support team.
  • Share the transaction reference and payment details requested for verification.

Our team will investigate the transaction with the relevant payment service provider. Eligible refunds will be initiated accordingly.

Duplicate successful payments for the same order will be reviewed and eligible excess payments will be refunded.

7. Incorrect, Damaged or Missing Products

If a customer receives an incorrect, damaged or incomplete order, they should report the issue as soon as reasonably possible.

Customers may be asked to provide:

  • Order ID.
  • Description of the issue.
  • Photographs or other supporting evidence, where relevant.

RailQuick will review the complaint and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, refund or another suitable remedy.

8. Refund Method

Approved refunds will ordinarily be processed through the original payment method / source account via our payment partner, including PhonePe Payment Gateway. RailQuick does not request customers to disclose their UPI PIN, card CVV, OTP or banking passwords to process refunds.

9. How to Request a Refund

Customers can contact us using the details below:

Customer Support: support@railquick.in
General Enquiries: contact@railquick.in
Website: https://www.railquickapp.com / railquickapp.in

Please include your Order ID, registered mobile number and transaction reference, where available.

10. Policy Updates

RailQuick Private Limited reserves the right to update this policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with the revised effective date.
