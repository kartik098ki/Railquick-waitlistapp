1. About RailQuick

RailQuick is a railway passenger-focused essentials delivery platform designed to help passengers order essential products during their train journeys and receive deliveries at designated railway stations or other supported delivery locations.

Our initial operational focus is on selected railway stations in Delhi, India.

RailQuick aims to make railway travel more convenient by providing access to essential products through an online ordering platform.
